World history

A small working simulation, not a complete evolutionary model. Individuals independently seek water, food and rest, learn how effective different food sources are, age, reproduce and die. Plants regrow according to rainfall and soils. Animals feed and reproduce. There are no scripted technology unlocks, language, housing or farming yet; those require their own testable learning mechanisms.
