Combined experiment report
Expansive Worlds and Ecological Diagnostics release with visual clarity update. A working prototype with a world four times the former area, not a complete evolutionary model. Individuals independently seek water, food and rest, learn how effective different food sources are, age, reproduce and die. Weather changes rainfall and soil moisture, floods low riverbanks, and affects vegetation. Rivers and lakes contain a simplified renewable aquatic food stock. Inhabitants remember a small number of observed food sites and navigate around impassable terrain. Herbivores eat plants; predators hunt herbivores and can injure beings. The beings already use male/female sexes and adult mate proximity for reproduction. Ecological deaths are tracked; there is no automatic wildlife respawning. There are no scripted technology unlocks, language, housing or farming yet; those require their own testable learning mechanisms.
