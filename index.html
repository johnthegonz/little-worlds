World history

Living Geography release. A small working simulation, not a complete evolutionary model. Individuals independently seek water, food and rest, learn how effective different food sources are, age, reproduce and die. Weather changes rainfall and soil moisture, floods low riverbanks, and affects vegetation. Rivers and lakes contain a simplified renewable aquatic food stock. Inhabitants remember a small number of observed food sites and navigate around impassable terrain. Herbivores eat plants; predators hunt herbivores and can injure inhabitants. The human population already uses male/female sexes and adult mate proximity for reproduction. Ecological deaths are tracked; there is no automatic wildlife respawning. There are no scripted technology unlocks, language, housing or farming yet; those require their own testable learning mechanisms.
